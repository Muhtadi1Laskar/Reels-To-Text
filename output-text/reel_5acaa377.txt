If these five books don't turn you into an obsessive reader, then I don't think anything will
because all five of these books had me sitting down for hours on end just not being able to put
them down. The first one is my most recent read which I gave five stars. It's 600 pages Pachinko
by Min Jin Lee. Despite its length, I finished it in a couple of days even though I'm a pretty slow
reader. It's a beautiful story of a Korean family living in Japan in the 20th century. We follow
multiple generations of this family as they struggle to live in a country that hates them.
I think it was a beautiful story. I think it reads really well so you can get through it very quickly.
Highly recommend it. The second book that will very likely make you obsessed with reading would be
Blindness by Jose Saramago. It's about a town where people start turning blind for no apparent reason
and we follow a group of people that turn blind and are sent to a mental asylum by the government
together with other blind people. Dozens and dozens of these blind people have to figure out how to live
their life without seeing which is extremely difficult and outside the mental asylum everyone
else is also turning blind so society is completely collapsing. There's complete anarchy everywhere
and the author really goes deep into how human nature kind of resets to its caveman style when
things go really really wrong. The third book is a 600 page novel that I finish in about four days as
well. It is The Secret History by Donna Tartt. This novel is extremely popular but it's also very
polarizing because the characters in it are extremely unlikable in my opinion but that obviously doesn't
matter because characters don't need to be likable in order for the book to be really good and really
readable. We follow a group of very charismatic but very different group of elite college students at a
university in New England. They essentially have their own way of thinking and living but let me tell
you they're up to no good. The next book that I couldn't put down is a bit of a rogue shout for this
category of books but it is Anton Chekhov's Plays and although it's a bit weird to recommend plays to
someone who just wants to read more but I genuinely believe well-written plays are some of the best way
of getting back into reading or just reading more and especially Chekhov's Plays are very profound but also
written in a very simple and easygoing way so you can get through them very quickly. The final book
would be Passing by Nella Larson. I don't think this book is as well known as the other four on this list
but I enjoyed it just as much. It's a story of two women in the 1920s America. Both of them pass for white
and while one of them embraces it the other one doesn't and later on in life they meet and essentially
compare how their lives have been up until that point. And as you can imagine there's a lot of strange
feelings involved and I couldn't put it down because it's so well written but it's also only
120 pages so you can get through it very quickly. If you'd like more book recommendations like this
feel free to follow me.
